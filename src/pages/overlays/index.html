  • Spotify player

    TikTok + Twitch · Both

    Show what is playing on your Spotify right on your stream, with the song, the artist and the cover. It is a step in the song requests editor, and it can also put the songs your viewers ask for into your Spotify queue. Frills keeps your Spotify login (encrypted, and deleted when you press Disconnect), so it keeps working after you close the page. Frills’ own Spotify app lets in only a short list of people, so everyone else connects with a free Spotify app of their own: the editor shows the nine steps. It needs Spotify Premium.

    Open the Spotify step →

About the beta ones. Alerts, last gifts and goals read follows, gifts and likes from TikTok through the free Frills relay. If connecting says the relay needs an update, please check back soon. The test buttons in each editor always work, so you can design yours first. Top likers counts the likes it sees from the moment it connects. TikTok and Twitch chat together is new: it has been tried with practice chats and a couple of real streams, but not on a long LIVE yet, so tell us how it goes. Song requests read the LIVE chat through the same relay, and the practice box in the editor works without a LIVE. Songs only go to your Spotify queue with Spotify Premium and Spotify playing, using your own free Spotify app (Step 4 of the song requests editor). Frills keeps the Spotify login you give it on its server, encrypted, until you press Disconnect or it has not been used for 14 days.
