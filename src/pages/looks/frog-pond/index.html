Frog chat overlay for TikTok LIVE and Twitch

Frog pond is a free, frog-themed chat overlay for TikTok LIVE and Twitch. A frog peeks over the top of your chat bubbles, a pond scene sits in the corner and frog confetti pops now and then. You pick it in the editor, type your name and paste one link into OBS, Streamlabs or TikTok LIVE Studio. No account, nothing to install.

What is in Frog pond

A green pond: a frog peeks over the top of the bubbles, a pond scene sits in the corner, and frog confetti pops now and then.

  • A frog peeking over the top. Two frogs take turns looking over the edge of your chat bubbles.
  • A pond scene. It sits on the corner of the bubbles.
  • Frog confetti. Little frog things pop up now and then.
  • Fresh green colors. It starts from the Mint look, and you can change every color.

There is also the first version, Frog pond: a pond and a lily pad on the corner of the bubbles, with a few green sparkles.

How to use it

  1. Open the editor with Frog pond already picked.
  2. Type your TikTok @name, your Twitch channel, or both. Not LIVE yet? Type frills_demo to practice.
  3. Copy your link and add it to your streaming app as a Browser source. The setup guide shows each step, and the TikTok guide says how TikTok chat reaches the overlay.

Make it yours

The pictures and the colors are separate, so you can mix them. In the editor, open Decorations on the bubbles and change Decoration size, switch A friend peeking over the top, A scene on a corner and Sparkles on or off, choose which side the corner scene sits on, and choose who gets decorations: everyone, moderators only, or gift messages. Then change the look under it as much as you like.

Questions about Frog pond

Is the frog chat overlay free?

Yes. Every look and every pack in Frills is free, with no limits and no account.

Does it work with TikTok LIVE and Twitch?

Yes. For TikTok, type your @name in the editor and Frills reads your LIVE’s public chat for you. For Twitch, type your channel name. Choose Both to show TikTok and Twitch chat together in one overlay.

Can I use the frog pictures with a different look?

Yes. The pictures do not depend on the look. Pick any look for the colors, the font and the shape of the bubbles, then pick Frog pond under Decorations on the bubbles.
