Cute chat overlay looks for your LIVE

A chat overlay does not have to be a grey box. Pick a starting look, put a little friend on your chat bubbles, and change every color, font and corner until it is yours. Everything here is free, works for TikTok LIVE and Twitch, and needs no account. The chats below are practice chats with made-up names.

Starting looks

These are the looks you can start from. Each one sets the colors, the font, the shape of the box and how messages arrive, and you can change all of it. The home page shows every one next to the others.

  • Blueberry. A dark, calm box with a soft periwinkle accent. The one most people start from. Use this look (Blueberry)
  • Frost. Frosted glass: a see-through box with a soft blur, so your game shows through. Use this look (Frost)
  • Mint. An outline with no fill, a fresh green accent, and messages that slide in. Use this look (Mint)
  • Bubblegum. Pink and cyan on dark purple, with a glow around every bubble. Use this look (Bubblegum)
  • Butter. Sunny yellow bubbles with a hard shadow, like a sticker. Use this look (Butter)
  • Arcade. A pixel font and sharp corners, with messages that arrive in pixel steps. Use this look (Arcade)
  • Whisper. Text only, no box at all: big, round letters straight onto your scene. Use this look (Whisper)

Make it yours

  • Change the accent and box colors, the box opacity, the roundness and the edge, the font and the text size.
  • Put the name in a small pill on the edge of the bubble, or inside it.
  • Line the text up left, center or right.
  • Keep messages on screen so they never fade, or choose how long each one stays.
  • Reading both TikTok and Twitch? Add a small platform icon to every message. Here is how.
