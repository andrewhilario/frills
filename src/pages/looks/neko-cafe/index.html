Cat chat overlay for TikTok LIVE and Twitch

Neko cafe is a free, cat-themed chat overlay for TikTok LIVE and Twitch. Cats peek over the top of your chat bubbles, a kitten sits on a cushion in the corner and little cat things float by. You pick it in the editor, type your name and paste one link into OBS, Streamlabs or TikTok LIVE Studio. No account, nothing to install.

What is in Neko cafe

Pink and cozy: cats peek over your chat bubbles, with a kitten on a cushion in the corner and little cat things floating by.

  • Cats peeking over the top. Two cats take turns looking over the edge of your chat bubbles.
  • A kitten on a cushion. A scene that sits on the corner of the bubbles.
  • Little cat things. Yarn, fish, paws, hearts and stars float by.
  • Pink and cozy colors. It starts from the Bubblegum look, and you can change every color.

How to use it

  1. Open the editor with Neko cafe already picked.
  2. Type your TikTok @name, your Twitch channel, or both. Not LIVE yet? Type frills_demo to practice.
  3. Copy your link and add it to your streaming app as a Browser source. The setup guide shows each step, and the TikTok guide says how TikTok chat reaches the overlay.

Make it yours

The pictures and the colors are separate, so you can mix them. In the editor, open Decorations on the bubbles and change Decoration size, switch A friend peeking over the top, A scene on a corner and Sparkles on or off, choose which side the corner scene sits on, and choose who gets decorations: everyone, moderators only, or gift messages. Then change the look under it as much as you like.

Questions about Neko cafe

Is the cat chat overlay free?

Yes. Every look and every pack in Frills is free, with no limits and no account.

Does it work with TikTok LIVE and Twitch?

Yes. For TikTok, type your @name in the editor and Frills reads your LIVE’s public chat for you. For Twitch, type your channel name. Choose Both to show TikTok and Twitch chat together in one overlay.

Can I use the cat pictures with a different look?

Yes. The pictures do not depend on the look. Pick any look for the colors, the font and the shape of the bubbles, then pick Neko cafe under Decorations on the bubbles.
