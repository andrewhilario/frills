Finishing the login…

What to check

  1. The redirect address. In your Spotify app’s settings it must be exactly . Press Add, then Save.
  2. The Client ID. It must be the one from your app’s Settings page (not the Client secret).
  3. Your name and email under Settings → User management in the Spotify dashboard, with the exact email of your Spotify account.
  4. Spotify Premium on the account that made the Spotify app.

Fix what is wrong, then press Log in with Spotify again.
