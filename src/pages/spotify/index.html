Spotify connection test

A private page to check that Frills can talk to your Spotify. It shows what is playing, finds songs and adds one to your queue. Nothing here is public yet.

Before you start. Open Spotify on your phone or computer and play any song, because Spotify only lets Frills add to the queue while a device is active. You need Spotify Premium for that, and your Spotify email must be on the app’s allowlist.
