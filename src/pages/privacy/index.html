Privacy

Frills has no accounts and no database. Here is what that means in practice.

What stays on your device

The editor saves your settings and your last channel name in your browser’s local storage, so it remembers you next time. They never leave your device. Clear your browser data and they are gone.

What is in your link

Your overlay link carries your look settings and your channel name. That is how the overlay page knows what to draw. Anyone you share the link with can see them.

How chat is read

On Twitch, your browser or your streaming app connects straight to Twitch’s chat servers to read public chat. Twitch sees a connection from your address, the same as it does for any viewer.

Fonts and pictures

Fonts are served from Frills itself. Emote pictures come from Twitch’s image server. {{privacyPictures}}

Hosting, cookies and counting

Frills is hosted on Cloudflare, which, like any host, sees ordinary web request data such as your address and the pages you open. Frills itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics today. If that changes, this page will say so first.

Who we are not

Frills isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Twitch, TikTok or OBS. Those names belong to their owners.
