Set up any overlay in about 20 seconds

One short clip for each overlay, with the same few steps written out under it. Every overlay works the same way: pick a look, try it, type your name, copy one link into your streaming app. The clips have no sound and loop. They were recorded in the real editor with the practice name frills_demo, so the chat and the gifts you see are made up.

Chat Alerts Last gifts Goals Top likers

Chat

Your TikTok or Twitch chat as speech bubbles on your stream, in a look you choose.

  1. Pick a look

    Choose a starting look. Then change anything: colors, font, size, text alignment, pictures on the bubbles and the name tag.

  2. Type your name and press Connect

    Choose TikTok or Twitch and type your @name or channel. On TikTok, chat only shows while you are LIVE. To practice first, type frills_demo.

  3. Copy your link

    Press Copy link.

  4. Paste it into your streaming app

    Add a Browser source and paste the link. The setup guide has the size to use and what to do if nothing shows.

Alerts

A pop-up for each follow, gift and share on TikTok, or for each sub, gifted sub, cheer and raid on Twitch.

  1. Try the test buttons

    Press Test follow, Test gift and the others above the preview. You see every alert without being LIVE.

  2. Pick a look and write your words

    Choose a look, then change the words, the animation and the sound for each kind of alert.

  3. Type your name and press Connect

    Choose TikTok or Twitch and type your name. On Twitch, follow alerts also need one login: the alerts guide shows how.

  4. Copy your link and paste it into your streaming app

    Press Copy link, then add a Browser source and paste it.

Open the alerts editor

Last gifts

A list of your most recent TikTok gifts, with the person, the gift and its picture.

  1. Try the test buttons

    Press Add a gift or Add six gifts to see the list fill up.

  2. Pick a look and what the list shows

    Choose a look, then set how many gifts show, where new gifts go and the words.

  3. Type your name and press Connect

    Type your TikTok @name. Real gifts only arrive while you are LIVE.

  4. Copy your link and paste it into your streaming app

    Press Copy link, then add a Browser source and paste it.

Open the last gifts editor

Goals

A bar that fills as you gain followers, gift diamonds or likes, and celebrates when you reach the goal.

  1. Set your goal and try it

    Choose what it counts (followers, gifts or likes) and the number to reach. Press +10 followers and Reach the goal to see the bar fill and celebrate.

  2. Pick a look

    Choose a look, then change the colors, the words and the size.

  3. Type your name and press Connect

    Type your TikTok @name. The follower goal starts from your real follower count when you are LIVE.

  4. Copy your link and paste it into your streaming app

    Press Copy link, then add a Browser source and paste it.

Open the goals editor
