Twitch follows

Twitch only tells a logged-in streamer who follows them. Log in once here and keep this page open while you stream: it passes each new follower to your alerts, so the alert pops up. Nothing else on your Twitch is read.

Good to know. Follow alerts only work while this page is open (a tab in the background is fine). Twitch asks you to log in again every few hours, and this page tells you when. The other Twitch alerts (subs, gifted subs, bits and raids) need none of this.

1. Log in

Checking…

Twitch will ask if Frills may see who follows your channel. That is the only thing it asks for. The login stays in this browser and is never sent to Frills.

2. Listening for new followers

    A test follower goes through the same way a real one does, so you can see the alert in your overlay.

    3. Show them in your alerts

    In the alerts editor, pick Twitch, turn on Show Twitch follows in my alerts link, and copy the link into your streaming app.
