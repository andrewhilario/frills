Connect Spotify (optional)

Show the song that is playing on your Spotify on your stream, and let viewers add songs to your Spotify queue with !sr. Once it is connected you can close this page. You do not need this for the song list: viewers can ask for songs without it.

When you log in, Frills keeps your Spotify login on its server, encrypted, so the Spotify player works with this page closed. Disconnect deletes it at once, and it is deleted after 14 days without use.

Checking what is playing…

You can close this page now: Frills reads Spotify for you. Keep your song requests overlay open in your streaming app, because it is what passes your viewers’ !sr and !next on.

Try it as a viewer

    Having trouble?
    • Songs only go to your Spotify queue with Spotify Premium, and Spotify has to be open and playing on a phone or computer (it does not have to be this computer).
    • While Frills is in testing, Spotify lets only a short list of people log in. If Spotify says you are not registered, tell us on the feedback page. Spotify allows only a few people, so we can add you only if there is room.
    • Your Spotify password is never seen by Frills. Frills keeps your Spotify login on its server, encrypted, and uses it only to read what is playing, and (when you have switched that on) to add songs to your queue and skip. Disconnect deletes it at once, and it is deleted after 14 days without use. You can also remove Frills at spotify.com/account/apps. Only the song’s name, artist, cover and position reach your overlay.
    • If the song on your stream stops updating, check that Spotify is playing and that your song requests overlay is open in your streaming app. If this step says your login ran out, log in again.
