Connect Spotify (optional)

Show the song that is playing on your Spotify on your stream, and let viewers add songs to your Spotify queue with !sr. Once it is connected you can close this page. You do not need this for the song list: viewers can ask for songs without it.

Before you start

  • You need Spotify Premium on the Spotify account you log in with. Spotify only lets Premium accounts add songs to a queue, and it only lets an app like this one work for a Premium account. A free Spotify account will not work, even with your own app. Check your plan
  • You make your own Spotify app, once. It is free and takes about five minutes: nine short steps below. Frills’ own Spotify app lets in only a short list of people, so this is the way for almost everyone.
Steps 1 to 7: make your own Spotify app

  1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard and log in with the Spotify account that has Premium (the one you stream with). If Spotify asks you to accept its terms or confirm your email, do that first.

    Open the dashboard

    You should see: a page called Dashboard with a Create app button.

  2. Press Create app.

    You should see: a form asking for an app name.

  3. Fill in the first boxes. App name: anything, for example Frills. App description: anything, for example Song requests. Website: leave it empty.

  4. Add the Redirect URI. Copy this address with the button, paste it into the Redirect URIs box and press Add.

    You should see: the address in a list under the box. If it is not in the list, it was not added. One wrong letter, or a missing slash at the end, and Spotify refuses the login.

  5. Tick Web API, agree to the terms and press Save. The question is “Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?”: tick Web API.

    You should see: your new app’s page.

  6. Add yourself as a user. This is the step most people miss. On your app’s page press Settings, open the User management tab (Spotify’s own help calls it Users Management), press Add new user, type your name and the exact email of your Spotify account, and press Add user.

    Spotify only lets the people on this list use an app that is still being tested, so add yourself even though it is your own app. Your email is shown at spotify.com/account/overview. You should see your name and email in the list.

  7. Copy the Client ID and paste it here. In Settings, under Basic information, copy the line called Client ID. It is 32 letters and numbers. Do not copy the Client secret: Frills never asks for it.

Steps 8 and 9: connect

  1. Press Log in with Spotify (the button just below these steps). If you were already connected before, press Disconnect first.

  2. On Spotify’s page, press Agree. You come back to this page by yourself.

    You should see: “Connected to Spotify as” and your name, just below. If it says something else, open It did not work? Find what you saw, further down this step.

When you log in, Frills keeps your Spotify login on its server, encrypted, so the Spotify player works with this page closed. Disconnect deletes it at once, and it is deleted after 14 days without use.

Checking what is playing…

You can close this page now: Frills reads Spotify for you. Keep your song requests overlay open in your streaming app, because it is what passes your viewers’ !sr and !next on.

Try it as a viewer

    It did not work? Find what you saw
    Spotify’s own page says “INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URI”
    Step 4. The address was not added, or not saved. Copy it again with the Copy button, paste it in the Redirect URIs box, press Add, then press Save at the bottom of the settings.
    Spotify’s own page says “INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid client”
    Step 7. The Client ID is wrong. Copy it again from the app’s Settings page, from the line called Client ID, and press Use my app.
    Frills says “Spotify said no” after you log in
    Two usual reasons. Step 6: your name and the exact email of your Spotify account must be in the app’s Settings → User management list. Premium: the Spotify account that made the app must have Premium. Fix it, then press Log in with Spotify again.
    It says connected, but viewers’ songs do not reach the queue
    Check these four things. (1) The switch Add songs my viewers ask for to my Spotify queue is on. (2) The line under the switches says your song requests overlay is open: if it says Frills cannot see it, the overlay is not open in your streaming app, or its link was copied before you connected Spotify (copy it again in step 5 and replace the old one). (3) Spotify is open and playing on a phone or computer (it does not have to be this computer). (4) Try it in Try it as a viewer below: its answer says what Spotify said.
    It worked, and then it stopped
    The most common reason is that the Premium on the account that made the app ended: Spotify turns such apps off. Renew it, then log in again. If only the song stopped updating, check that Spotify is playing and that your song requests overlay is open.

    Your Spotify password is never seen by Frills. Frills keeps your Spotify login on its server, encrypted, and uses it only to read what is playing, and (when you have switched that on) to add songs to your queue and skip. Disconnect deletes it at once, and it is deleted after 14 days without use. You can also remove Frills at spotify.com/account/apps. Only the song’s name, artist, cover and position reach your overlay.
