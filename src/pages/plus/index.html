What you get

Natural AI Pet Voices & AI Co-Host (Twitch + TikTok). Each month comes with an allowance of 150,000 characters of AI voice (about 3,000 spoken chat messages, and your account page shows how much is left). You can cast one AI voice for chat commands and another for subs, bits, raids, or gifts, restrict chat triggers to Twitch subscribers, VIPs, or mods, and switch on AI Pet Co-Host so your pet reacts with short in-character replies to gifts and viewer !ask questions. When an allowance runs out or Frills Plus ends, the pet keeps talking with free browser voices.

Upload your own 2- or 3-frame Talking Pet mascot. Turn any Etsy or custom PNGTuber / mascot PNG, WebP, or GIF into a reactive stream pet in 30 seconds: upload idle, mouth-open talking, and excited gift/sub frames in the Talking Pet editor and Frills hosts them cleanly inside your overlay.

1-Click Live Rebrand & Phone Stream Deck controls. Save up to 50 permanent /o/… cloud overlays synced to your account. Switch every overlay in OBS to a new aesthetic at once from your account page, trigger remote timer adjustments, live TTS announcements, jar drops, goal increments, or board resets from your phone, and keep OBS working without re-copying links even if Plus later ends.

Crash-proof Cloud Memory & Weekly / Monthly leaderboards. Stateful overlays (Subathon Timer, Gift Jar, Goal Bar, Top Gifters, and Top Likers) back up their live progress to the cloud automatically so a crash mid-subathon or multi-week campaign does not reset your totals.

Zero-drop TikTok LIVE connection & priority relay. Free TikTok overlays share a capped community relay. Frills Plus gives your TikTok overlays dedicated 24/7 cloud slots using one revocable overlay code on your account page, with automatic fallback to the free relay so your stream stays live.

Everything that is free today stays free, with no account. Frills Plus does not take any free feature away.

Price and billing

Frills Plus is $5.99 a month; the final total including any local sales tax or VAT is shown on the checkout page before you pay. It renews every month until you cancel. You sign in with an email link first, so the payment is tied to your account and not to a key you could lose.

Frills Plus is sold through Polar, which acts as Merchant of Record, takes the payment, charges any sales tax or VAT and sends your receipt. For now it is sold in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, the European Union and EFTA countries, Australia and New Zealand. The free browser voices and free overlays work everywhere.

Cancelling

Sign in on the account page and press Manage or cancel. It opens your billing portal at Polar, where you can also change your card and see your receipts. If you cancel, Frills Plus stays on until the end of the month you already paid for, then stops and does not charge you again. You do not have to write to us.

Refunds

If Frills Plus is not for you, tell us within 14 days of a payment and we will refund that payment in full. A full refund turns Frills Plus off straight away. Nothing on this page takes away any rights you have by law where you live.
