What you get

An always-on cloud TikTok LIVE server. Free TikTok overlays share one capped community relay. Frills Plus routes your TikTok overlays (chat, alerts, gift jar, goals, top likers, song requests, subathon timer and talking pet) through a dedicated 24/7 cloud server reserved for Plus accounts, using short-lived signed tickets. It is built for high reliability, and if the Plus server ever restarts or is unreachable — or if your subscription ends — your overlays fall back to the free relay automatically so your stream stays live.

Live-updating cloud overlay links. Save up to 50 named presets across all twelve Frills editors, synced to your account. Each saved overlay gets one permanent link (/o/…) that you paste into OBS Studio or TikTok LIVE Studio once — whenever you change themes, fonts, alert sounds or rules and press Save, your stream updates live within seconds without re-copying URLs. If Frills Plus ends, your saved links keep showing your last saved look so your stream never breaks.

AI voices for the talking pet. More natural voices than the ones built into your browser. Each month comes with an allowance of 150,000 characters of AI voice, counted in characters of the words spoken (a short chat message is about 50), and your account page shows how much you have left. When the allowance runs out, or Frills Plus ends, the pet carries on with the free browser voice. It never goes quiet.

Everything that is free today stays free, with no account. Frills Plus does not take any free feature away.

Price and billing

Frills Plus is $5.99 a month; the final total including any local sales tax or VAT is shown on the checkout page before you pay. It renews every month until you cancel. You sign in with an email link first, so the payment is tied to your account and not to a key you could lose.

Frills Plus is sold through Polar, which acts as Merchant of Record, takes the payment, charges any sales tax or VAT and sends your receipt. For now it is sold in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, the European Union and EFTA countries, Australia and New Zealand. The free browser voices and free overlays work everywhere.

Cancelling

Sign in on the account page and press Manage or cancel. It opens your billing portal at Polar, where you can also change your card and see your receipts. If you cancel, Frills Plus stays on until the end of the month you already paid for, then stops and does not charge you again. You do not have to write to us.

Refunds

If Frills Plus is not for you, tell us within 14 days of a payment and we will refund that payment in full. A full refund turns Frills Plus off straight away. Nothing on this page takes away any rights you have by law where you live.

Terms of use

Frills Plus is for your own live streams. Do not share or resell your Plus overlay code, abuse the dedicated relay or AI voice service, or use Frills for anything unlawful. Because TikTok offers no official chat API for overlays, we work to keep the dedicated cloud server running reliably (with automatic fallback to the free relay), but we cannot guarantee uninterrupted service if a streaming platform changes how its chat works.

Your data

An account keeps your email address and what you bought, and nothing else about you. The privacy page lists exactly what, including how sign-in emails are delivered and where the words of an AI voice go. You can delete your account yourself on the account page.
