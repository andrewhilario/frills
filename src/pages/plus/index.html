Frills Plus

Frills stays free. Frills Plus is a paid extra for things that cost money to run. Right now that is AI voices for the talking pet. Here is what it is, how to cancel and how refunds work.

What you get

AI voices for the talking pet: more natural voices than the ones built into your browser. Each month comes with an allowance of AI voice, counted in characters of the words spoken (a short chat message is about 50), and your account page shows how much you have left. When the allowance runs out, or Frills Plus ends, the pet carries on with the free browser voice. It never goes quiet.

Everything that is free today stays free, with no account. Frills Plus does not take any free feature away.

Price and billing

The price, how often it is charged and any tax are shown on the checkout page before you pay. It renews every month until you cancel. You sign in with an email link first, so the payment is tied to your account and not to a key you could lose.

Frills Plus is sold through Lemon Squeezy, which takes the payment, charges any sales tax or VAT and sends your receipt. For now it is sold in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, the European Union and EFTA countries, Australia and New Zealand. The free browser voices work everywhere.

Cancelling

Sign in on the account page and press Manage or cancel. It opens your billing page at Lemon Squeezy, where you can also change your card and see your receipts. If you cancel, Frills Plus stays on until the end of the month you already paid for, then stops and does not charge you again. You do not have to write to us.

Refunds

If Frills Plus is not for you, tell us within 14 days of a payment and we will refund that payment in full. A full refund turns Frills Plus off straight away. Nothing on this page takes away any rights you have by law where you live.
