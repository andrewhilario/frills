Step-by-step setup guide

Every Frills Plus feature works right in your browser, OBS Studio, and TikTok LIVE Studio with nothing to install:

1. Dedicated 24/7 Cloud TikTok LIVE Server & Overlay Code

  1. Sign in on your account page with your Frills Plus email.
  2. Inside the Dedicated TikTok server & overlay code section, click Activate on this browser.
  3. Open any overlay editor (Chat, Alerts, Gift jar, Goals, Subathon timer, Talking pet, Top gifters, Top likers) or Quick start and copy your link (or save it to the cloud). Your Plus code is included automatically so your TikTok stream routes through the dedicated 24/7 cloud server with automatic fallback to the free relay.

2. Live Cloud Overlays (/o/…) & 1-Click Global Theme Sync

  1. Open any overlay editor, pick your starting look, and enter your TikTok or Twitch channel name.
  2. Scroll to the final Add it to your stream step. Inside the Live cloud link box right under Copy link, type a name (such as Main Chat) and click Save to cloud.
  3. Copy your permanent /o/… link and paste it once as a Browser Source in OBS Studio or a Link Source in TikTok LIVE Studio.
  4. To change your stream’s entire aesthetic while live, open your account page, pick a theme in the top bar (Matcha Frog, Neko Café, Bunny Cloud, Cozy Night, etc.), and click Apply theme to all overlays. Every open /o/… source in OBS updates within 3 seconds without pasting a new URL.

3. 1-Link Master Stream Scene (16:9 OBS & 9:16 TikTok LIVE Studio)

  1. Save the overlays you want in your layout (for example, Chat, Alerts, Gift Jar, and Subathon Timer) using Save to cloud in their editors.
  2. Go to your account page and scroll to 1-Link Master Stream Scene inside the Live cloud overlays & remote deck card.
  3. Choose 16:9 Landscape (OBS / Twitch) or 9:16 Vertical (TikTok LIVE Studio) and pick a screen zone for each overlay (Top-Left, Top-Center, Top-Right, Mid-Left, Center, Mid-Right, Bottom-Left, Bottom-Center, Bottom-Right, or Hide).
  4. Click Save 1-Link Master Scene, press Copy scene link, and paste that single /o/… URL into OBS (1920×1080) or TikTok LIVE Studio (1080×1920). Any zone or theme changes you save later update live on stream.

4. Live Phone Stream Deck & Crash-Proof Cloud Memory

  1. Keep your /o/… cloud overlay (or 1-Link Master Scene) open in OBS Studio or TikTok LIVE Studio on your streaming PC.
  2. On your phone, open frills.valwidgets.live/account/ in Safari or Chrome and sign in with your same email. (Tip: tap Add to Home Screen on iOS or Android to launch it like a dedicated Stream Deck app.)
  3. Scroll to Live cloud overlays & remote deck and tap the remote buttons under any saved overlay:
    • Subathon Timer: Start / Resume, Pause, +5m, −1m, Reset
    • Talking Pet TTS: Type a live announcement and tap Speak Live or Stop Voice
    • Alerts: Test Gift Alert, Test Sub Alert
    • Gift Jar: +100 Drop, Fill Jar, Empty Jar
    • Goal Bar: +1, +10, Reset Goal
    • Chat & Leaderboards: Clear Chat, Reset Board
  4. While your /o/… link runs in OBS, your Gift Jar, Goal Bar, Subathon Timer, Top Gifters, and Top Likers automatically checkpoint their live totals to the cloud so an OBS crash or PC restart never resets your progress.

5. Talking Pet Neural AI Voices (150,000 chars/mo) & Custom 3-Frame PNGTuber

  1. After clicking Activate on this browser on your account page, open the Talking Pet TTS editor.
  2. In Step 2 (Voice & speech), pick your neural AI voice for viewer !say chat messages and a separate AI voice for gifts, subs, bits, or raids (you can also filter !say to Twitch subscribers, VIPs, or moderators).
  3. To upload your own mascot, choose Your own PNGTuber under Pet character in Step 1 and upload up to 3 images (PNG, WebP, or GIF): Idle, Talking (mouth open), and Excited (gift / sub alert).
  4. Save to cloud or copy your link into OBS. Track your remaining monthly AI characters (150,000 / month) anytime on your account page; if the monthly allowance runs out, your pet automatically falls back to free browser voices.

6. ★ VIP Jackpot Alert Tiers

  1. Open the Alerts editor and make sure Golden VIP spotlight on big gifts, big cheers & sub bombs is ticked in Step 1 (Pick a look).
  2. In Step 3 (Each alert), open Gifts (TikTok), Gifted subs (Twitch), or Bits (Twitch) and upload your custom VIP image, GIF, and sound under the big-gift, 20+ gifted subs, or big-cheer uploaders.
  3. Click Big gift or Big cheer in the top preview bar to preview the golden ★ VIP badge and spotlight ring, then save to cloud or copy your link.

7. 1-Click Post-Stream “Hall of Fame” MVP Recap Card

  1. At the end of your stream, open your account page and scroll to 1-Click Stream MVP Recap Card inside Live cloud overlays & remote deck.
  2. Your channel name, #1 Top Gifter / Sub, #1 Top Liker, and Gift Jar / Goal progress auto-fill from your live cloud checkpoints (you can also edit any field before downloading).
  3. Choose 9:16 Vertical (TikTok Story / Reels) or 16:9 Landscape (Discord / X) and pick a theme (Cream & Gold, Midnight Arcade, or Neon Bubblegum).
  4. Click Download Recap PNG to save your Hall of Fame card and post it to TikTok Stories, Discord, or X.

Cancelling

Sign in on the account page and press Manage or cancel. It opens your billing portal at Polar, where you can also change your card and see your receipts. If you cancel, Frills Plus stays on until the end of the month you already paid for, then stops and does not charge you again. You do not have to write to us.
