    Step-by-step: How to set up every Frills Plus feature
    1. 24/7 Cloud TikTok Server & AI Voices: Click Activate on this browser above, then open Talking pet (to pick neural AI voices or upload your 3-frame PNGTuber) or any editor to route TikTok through the dedicated server.
    2. Save a Cloud Overlay (/o/…): In any editor (Chat, Alerts, Gift jar, Subathon timer, Goal bar, Talking pet, Top gifters, Top likers), scroll to the final step and press Create live link inside the Live cloud link box.
    3. 1-Link Master Stream Scene: Once you save at least 1 cloud overlay, use 1-Link Master Stream Scene below to pick 16:9 (OBS) or 9:16 (TikTok LIVE Studio), assign screen zones, and paste that one master /o/… link into your streaming app.
    4. Phone Stream Deck & Theme Sync: Open frills.valwidgets.live/account/ on your phone while your /o/… link is open in OBS to tap +5m Timer, Speak Live, Test Gift Alert, Fill Jar, or Apply theme to all overlays live.
    5. ★ VIP Jackpot Alerts & Post-Stream MVP Card: Upload custom big-gift/big-cheer media in Step 3 of Alerts, and click Download Recap PNG below after your stream to share your 9:16 or 16:9 Hall of Fame card. Full guide.
