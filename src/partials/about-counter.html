About this overlay

A live counter is a see-through web page that shows how many people are watching your TikTok LIVE and how many likes it has, changing as they do. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick a look and press the test buttons to watch the numbers go up.
  2. Choose what shows: the viewers, the likes, or both, side by side, one under the other, or in one small pill.
  3. Type your TikTok name.
  4. Copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • The viewer count is TikTok’s own number, sent every few seconds, so it can be a few seconds behind. Until TikTok has said it, the viewers stay hidden instead of showing a wrong 0.
  • The likes are the LIVE’s total, as TikTok counts them. If you open the overlay in the middle of a LIVE, it shows the total from the first like it sees.
  • It is for TikTok LIVE only, because Twitch has no likes. It is in beta.

Where does the viewer count come from?

From TikTok itself, through the free Frills relay, the same way the follower count and likes arrive. It shows what TikTok says; it does not count anything on its own.

Does the counter work on Twitch?

No. Twitch has no likes, and its viewer count needs a Twitch login that Frills does not ask for. It is for TikTok LIVE only.
