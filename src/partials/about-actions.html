About this overlay

Frills Actions turns live TikTok and Twitch chat commands, gifts, follows, shares, likes, subs, bits, and raids into real-time stream automations, VTube Studio named sticky notes & props, and on-screen effects—directly from one Browser source with zero extra software to install. Up to 10 actions and up to 5 live VTube Studio sticky notes on your face at once are completely free with no account; Frills Plus unlocks unlimited actions, unlimited sticky notes piling up, all other VTube Studio actions (hotkeys, custom props, expressions, zooms, tints), live cloud updates, and Phone Stream Deck buttons.

Supported streaming apps (not for TikTok LIVE Studio)

  • OBS Studio (version 28 or newer) — full support: runs every action, including OBS Replay Buffer clips (!clip), instant Replay Cam (!replay), scene switches, source toggles, webcam filters, mic mutes, local media videos, text updates, OBS hotkeys, built-in sounds & banners, and VTube Studio actions (up to 5 named sticky notes free; unlimited + all other VTS actions with Frills Plus).
  • Streamlabs Desktop — Browser source & VTube Studio actions: runs built-in sounds, banners, wait timers, free VTube Studio named sticky notes (up to 5 at once), and all Frills Plus VTube Studio actions (unlimited sticky notes, hotkeys, props, expressions, avatar zoom/flip, color tints). Because Streamlabs Desktop does not include OBS Studio’s WebSocket v5 server on port 4455, OBS-specific steps (!clip, !replay, OBS scenes, filters, mutes, and OBS hotkeys) require OBS Studio.
  • TikTok LIVE Studio — not supported: Frills Actions is built exclusively for OBS Studio and Streamlabs Desktop and will not work in TikTok LIVE Studio.

Step-by-step setup for OBS Studio

  1. In OBS Studio (version 28 or newer), open Tools → WebSocket Server Settings, tick Enable WebSocket server (port 4455 by default), and click Show Connect Info if you enabled authentication. For !clip and !replay, also open Settings → Output → Replay Buffer and tick Enable Replay Buffer.
  2. In Step 2 above, enter your OBS port and password (if enabled) and click Connect OBS. Frills reads your live scenes, sources, audio inputs, filters, and hotkeys into dropdown suggestions automatically. If you want instant replays, click Create Replay Cam once.
  3. Optional (Frills Plus): To trigger VTube Studio sticky notes, props, or expressions, open VTube Studio → Settings → General → Start API (port 8001), click Connect VTube Studio in Step 2, and press Allow on the popup inside VTube Studio.
  4. In Step 3, tap any starter pack or + Blank action to build your WHEN → DO rules. Press Test on any rule to watch it fire live in OBS or VTube Studio before going live.
  5. In Step 4, choose TikTok, Twitch, or Both and enter your channel name. In Step 5, click Copy link.
  6. In OBS Studio, go to Sources → + → Browser, name it Frills Actions, paste your full copied link (keeping everything after #), set Width / Height to match your canvas (1920 × 1080 landscape or 1080 × 1920 vertical), and tick Control audio via OBS if your actions play sounds.

Step-by-step setup for Streamlabs Desktop

  1. Skip the OBS WebSocket section in Step 2—Streamlabs Desktop does not run OBS Studio’s WebSocket v5 server on port 4455.
  2. Optional (Frills Plus): If you use VTube Studio, open VTube Studio → Settings → General → Start API (port 8001), click Connect VTube Studio in Step 2, and press Allow on the one-time popup inside VTube Studio.
  3. In Step 3, switch off or delete any OBS-only starter rules (!clip and !replay) and add VTube Studio, Play sound, or Show banner actions (such as + VTS Sticky note or + VTS Face zoom). Press Test on each rule to confirm it fires on your avatar or preview.
  4. In Step 4, choose TikTok, Twitch, or Both and enter your channel name. In Step 5, click Copy link (keep everything after #, including #vtsToken=…, intact).
  5. In Streamlabs Desktop, open your scene’s Sources panel, click +, pick Browser Source, and click Add Source.
  6. Name it Frills Actions, paste your copied link into the URL box, set Width and Height to match your canvas (1920 × 1080 landscape or 1080 × 1920 vertical), enable Control audio via Streamlabs if you use sound steps, and click Close.

Good to know

  • Actions is made for OBS Studio and Streamlabs Desktop only—do not add this overlay to TikTok LIVE Studio. For general setup tips, see the OBS setup guide and the test guide.
  • Your OBS WebSocket password and VTube Studio token stay on your computer inside the URL’s # hash fragment—browsers never send hash fragments to Frills or Cloudflare servers.
  • Timed actions (like switching scenes, enabling a webcam filter, muting your mic for 10 seconds, or pinning a VTube Studio sticky note for 30 seconds) automatically revert when the timer finishes, and queued rules run in order so overlapping gifts never fight each other.
  • Combine Actions with Spin the wheel, Gift jar, Alerts, or Subathon timer from the overlays hub.

Why will Actions not work in TikTok LIVE Studio?

Frills Actions requires a standard desktop Browser source with local WebSocket access for OBS Studio (port 4455) and VTube Studio (port 8001). TikTok LIVE Studio does not expose a local control API or Replay Buffer, and its built-in web source is not supported for Actions—use OBS Studio or Streamlabs Desktop instead.

Which actions work in Streamlabs Desktop vs. OBS Studio?

In OBS Studio (28+), all 17 step types work—including !clip, !replay, scene switches, source visibility, webcam filters, mic mute, media playback, OBS text, OBS hotkeys, VTube Studio actions, sounds, and banners. In Streamlabs Desktop, Browser source actions—VTube Studio props/sticky notes/expressions/movements/tints (Frills Plus), Play sound, Show banner, and Wait—work directly; OBS WebSocket steps require OBS Studio.

How many actions and sticky notes are free?

You can create and run up to 10 actions and up to 5 live VTube Studio sticky notes on your face at once completely free with no account required (when a 6th gift arrives on Free, the oldest sticky note peels off so the new gifter’s name appears right away). Frills Plus removes the 10-action limit, lets unlimited sticky notes pile up on your avatar, and unlocks live execution for all other VTube Studio actions (hotkeys, custom props, expressions, zoom/flip, color tints) plus cloud syncing and Phone Stream Deck triggers.

Are my OBS password and VTube Studio token sent to Frills?

No. Frills stores both only in your browser’s local storage and puts them after the # hash symbol in your Browser source URL. By browser standard, nothing after # is ever transmitted in HTTP requests to any server, and your Browser source connects only to OBS or VTube Studio on your own PC.

Can viewers stick sticky notes with their name onto my VTube Studio avatar?

Yes—for free (up to 5 on your face at once) in both OBS Studio and Streamlabs Desktop, with zero manual setup. Connect VTube Studio in Step 2 (Settings → General → Start API, port 8001), press Allow on the popup, and tap + VTS Sticky note in Step 3. Whenever a qualifying gift lands, Frills draws a cute sticky note with the viewer’s name and gift and pins it right onto your avatar’s face so it moves with your head (click Pick in VTS if you want to choose an exact forehead or cheek spot). Frills Plus lets unlimited sticky notes stack on your avatar and unlocks all other VTube Studio actions.

Can viewers spam !clip or !replay?

No. Every action has its own cooldown timer (in seconds), command actions can be locked to Mods & streamer or Streamer only, and queued actions wait their turn cleanly instead of overlapping.
