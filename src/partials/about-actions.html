How to use Frills Actions

Frills Actions does something on your stream when something happens on your live. Each action has a WHEN (what to watch for, such as a gift, a follow, or a chat command) and a DO (up to 5 steps, such as saving a clip, switching a scene, playing a sound, showing a banner, or pinning the viewer’s name on your avatar as a sticky note). For example: WHEN someone sends a gift, DO pin a sticky note with their name on your VTube Studio avatar. It works with TikTok LIVE and Twitch, from one Browser source, with nothing to install.

What you need

  • OBS Studio 28 or newer or Streamlabs Desktop. TikTok LIVE Studio is not supported.
  • VTube Studio on the same computer, only if you want sticky notes or other avatar effects.
  • Your TikTok or Twitch channel name.
  • No account and no download. Up to 10 actions and up to 5 sticky notes on your avatar at once are free.

Set it up in 6 steps

  1. Pick a look (Step 1 above). Choose whether a small on-screen message appears when an action runs, and how long it stays. The defaults are fine.
  2. Connect OBS Studio or Streamlabs Desktop (Step 2). Pick your app under Streaming app. In OBS Studio, open Tools → WebSocket Server Settings, tick Enable WebSocket server (port 4455), paste your password if set, and click Connect OBS. In Streamlabs Desktop, open Settings → Mobile (or Remote Control), click the QR code, click Show Details, copy the API Token (port 59650), and click Connect Streamlabs. For automatic !clip, also turn on Settings → Output → Replay Buffer → Enable Replay Buffer.
  3. Connect VTube Studio (Step 2). Only for sticky notes and avatar effects. In VTube Studio, open Settings → General and turn on Start API (port 8001). On this page, click Connect VTube Studio, then press Allow on the popup inside VTube Studio. The first sticky note may ask for one more permission, Load custom images as items. Allow it so Frills can draw the viewer’s name.
  4. Choose your actions (Step 3). Tap a starter such as + !clip, + !hype banner, + Gift → Replay, or + VTS Sticky note, or tap + Blank action. Open an action to change its WHEN (the trigger) and its DO steps. Each action also has a cooldown so it cannot be spammed.
  5. Connect your stream (Step 4). Choose TikTok, Twitch, or Both and type your channel name. This is where Frills listens for gifts, follows, and chat commands.
  6. Add it to your streaming app (Step 5). Click Copy link. In OBS Studio, go to Sources → + → Browser. In Streamlabs Desktop, go to Sources → + → Browser Source. Name it Frills Actions and paste the whole link, keeping everything after the #. Set Width and Height to match your canvas (1920 × 1080 landscape or 1080 × 1920 vertical). Tick Control audio via OBS (or via Streamlabs) if your actions play sounds. Keep OBS and VTube Studio open while you stream.

Try it before you go live

Press Test on any action in Step 3, or use the buttons under the preview (Test !clip, Test !replay, Test sticky note, Simulate gift). The Live activity log next to the preview shows what ran and any error. A step that uses OBS or VTube Studio needs that app connected first. To try gifts and commands without going live, type frills_demo as your TikTok name in Step 4.

Three good first actions

  • Save a clip when a mod types !clip. Tap + !clip. It saves the last moments from OBS’s Replay Buffer, and only mods and you can trigger it.
  • Pin the gifter’s name on your avatar. Tap + VTS Sticky note. It starts with gifts worth 100 diamonds or more, so open the action and lower the number if you want smaller gifts to count. Press Clear sticky notes in Step 2 to wipe them.
  • A hype banner anyone can start. Tap + !hype banner. Viewers type !hype and a banner with confetti and a sound appears.

If something does not work

  • “Not connected” for OBS: in OBS, open Tools → WebSocket Server Settings and make sure the server is enabled, OBS is version 28 or newer, and the port and password match what you typed here.
  • “Could not reach VTube Studio”: turn on Settings → General → Start API (allow plugins) in VTube Studio and check the port is 8001.
  • “Permission denied in VTube Studio”: click Connect VTube Studio again and press Allow on the popup inside VTube Studio.
  • The sticky note does not show your viewer’s name: allow Load custom images as items in VTube Studio, and make sure your avatar model is loaded.
  • Test works but nothing happens live: check the channel name in Step 4, paste the whole link again including everything after the #, and make sure the Browser source is in the scene you are streaming.
  • No sound: tick Control audio via OBS (or Streamlabs) on the Browser source.
  • No clips or replays: turn on the Replay Buffer in OBS (Settings → Output → Replay Buffer), or click Start Replay Buffer in Step 2.

Which apps work

  • OBS Studio (28+): full support. Every step type works, including !clip, !replay, scene switches, source visibility, webcam filters, mic mutes, media playback, text updates, OBS hotkeys, sounds, banners, and VTube Studio actions.
  • Streamlabs Desktop: native remote control + Browser source steps. Automatic !clip (Save Replay Buffer), auto-start Replay Buffer, scene switches, show/hide source, audio mute, sounds, banners, waits, and VTube Studio sticky notes (up to 5 at once free) all work via Streamlabs’ built-in Remote Control API (port 59650). Instant Replay Cam, source filters, media/text updates, and OBS hotkeys remain OBS Studio only.
  • TikTok LIVE Studio: not supported. Actions needs a normal desktop Browser source with local access to OBS (port 4455) and VTube Studio (port 8001). TikTok LIVE Studio has no local control API or Replay Buffer.

Good to know

  • Your OBS password and VTube Studio token stay on your computer, inside the # part of the link. Browsers never send that part to Frills or any server, and your Browser source talks only to OBS and VTube Studio on your own PC.
  • Timed actions (switching a scene, enabling a webcam filter, muting your mic for 10 seconds, or pinning a sticky note for 30 seconds) switch back by themselves when the timer ends. Queued actions run in order so overlapping gifts never fight each other.
  • For general setup tips, see the OBS setup guide and the test guide. Actions works well with Spin the wheel, Gift jar, Alerts, and the Subathon timer from the overlays hub.

How many actions and sticky notes are free?

You can create and run up to 10 actions, and up to 5 sticky notes on your avatar at once, completely free with no account. When a 6th sticky note arrives on Free, the oldest one peels off so the new gifter’s name shows right away. Frills Plus raises the 10-action limit, lets unlimited sticky notes pile up, adds custom sticker shapes, and unlocks the other VTube Studio actions (hotkeys, props, expressions, zoom and flip, color tints) plus cloud syncing and Phone Stream Deck triggers.

Can viewers get their name on my VTube Studio avatar?

Yes, for free, in both OBS Studio and Streamlabs Desktop. Connect VTube Studio in Step 2, press Allow, and tap + VTS Sticky note in Step 3. When a gift that qualifies arrives, Frills draws a sticky note with the viewer’s name and the gift and pins it to your avatar so it moves with your head. Click Pick in VTS if you want to choose an exact forehead or cheek spot.

Can viewers spam !clip or !replay?

No. Every action has its own cooldown (in seconds), command actions can be limited to Mods & streamer or Streamer only, and queued actions wait their turn instead of overlapping.
