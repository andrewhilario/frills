About this overlay

The stream commands & gear pop-up overlay lets your viewers ask about your setup, sensitivity, specs, or social links in chat (such as .mouse, .sens, !specs, .discord). A cozy on-screen card pops up with your answer, a mascot peeker from your chosen look pack, and an audio chime. It works on TikTok LIVE, Twitch, or both at once. 100% free, private, and requires zero logins or accounts.

How to use it

  1. Choose your favorite mascot pack (Frog Pond, Neko Cafe, Bunny Cloud, or Hamster Burrow) and audio chime.
  2. Customize your commands, triggers (e.g. mouse, sens, specs), and prefix (e.g. . or !).
  3. Connect your TikTok username, Twitch channel, or both.
  4. Copy your link into OBS Studio, Streamlabs, or TikTok LIVE Studio as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • Zero accounts & 100% private: All your custom commands are encoded right into your link. Nothing is stored on an external server or database.
  • Anti-spam cooldown: Protects your stream from chat spam. If five viewers type !mouse in a row, only the first one triggers the pop-up until the cooldown expires.
  • Custom prefix: You can use dots (.mouse), exclamation marks (!mouse), slashes (/mouse), or custom triggers.
  • Interactive test simulator: You can test any command right here in the editor using the test buttons or the live chat simulator.

Can viewers on both TikTok and Twitch use commands?

Yes! Choose Both in step 3 to connect both your TikTok LIVE and Twitch channel. Viewers on either platform can trigger the same commands on your stream.

Do I need an account or login to save my commands?

No! Frills never asks for an account. Your settings are remembered in your browser and saved directly into the Browser Source URL.
