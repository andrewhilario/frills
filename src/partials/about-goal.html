About this overlay

A goals overlay is a see-through web page with a progress bar for something you want to reach on your stream: TikTok followers, gift diamonds or likes, or Twitch subs, bits and follows—individually or across both platforms together. It shows the count and the target, cheers at milestones, celebrates when you get there, and can raise the next goal automatically. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick a look, a bar design (classic, candy stripes, blocks, hearts, stars, neon glow, thin line or a ring) and a goal type: followers, gifts, likes, subs or bits.
  2. Set your target, how big each next goal is, and the words and the sound for the celebration.
  3. Pick TikTok, Twitch, or Both and type your channel name(s).
  4. Copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • The follower goal can start from your account’s real follower count when TikTok tells the relay. Gift, like, sub and bit goals start from zero.
  • If the page reloads, the count starts again unless you turn on remember, which keeps it in your own browser or streaming app and nowhere else.
  • Works on TikTok LIVE, Twitch and combined Both multistream mode. The overlays questions explain how goals count.

What can the goals overlay count?

TikTok followers, gift diamonds or likes, and Twitch subs, gifted subs or bit cheers. You pick the goal type in Step 2.

What happens when I reach the goal?

The bar shows a celebration with the words you chose, and it can play a sound if you pick one (it is quiet until you do). It can also set the next goal for you, as big as you chose.

Does the follower goal start from my real follower count?

It can on TikTok, when TikTok tells the relay your follower count. Gift, like, sub and bit goals start from zero. If the page reloads the count starts again, unless you turn on remember, which keeps it in your own browser or streaming app and nowhere else.
