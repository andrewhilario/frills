About this overlay

A goals overlay is a see-through web page with a progress bar for something you want to reach on your LIVE: new followers, the diamonds of gifts, or likes. It shows the count and the target, celebrates when you get there, and can set the next goal for you. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick a look and a goal type: followers, gifts or likes.
  2. Set your target, how big each next goal is, and the words for the celebration.
  3. Type your TikTok name.
  4. Copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • The follower goal can start from your account’s real follower count when TikTok tells the relay. Gift and like goals start from zero.
  • If the page reloads, the count starts again unless you turn on remember, which keeps it in your own browser or streaming app and nowhere else.
  • It is for TikTok LIVE only for now, and it is in beta. The overlays questions explain how goals count.
