About this overlay

A top likers overlay is a see-through web page with a live ranking of the people who tapped the most likes on your LIVE, sliding into place as they tap. When someone passes a milestone (1,000 likes at the least), their picture, name and likes zoom in with a crown. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick a look and press the test buttons to watch the ranking move and a milestone celebration.
  2. Choose how many people show, whether to show likes and medals, and how often to celebrate.
  3. Type your TikTok name.
  4. Copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • It counts what it sees from the moment it connects. If the page reloads the ranking starts again, unless you turn on remember, which keeps it in your own browser or streaming app.
  • It is for TikTok LIVE only, because Twitch has no likes. It is in beta.
