About this overlay

A song requests overlay is a see-through web page that shows the songs your viewers asked for, one after the other, with their covers. Viewers type !sr and a song name in your TikTok LIVE chat. You or a moderator type !next to move to the next one. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick a look and press the test buttons to watch requests arrive and move along.
  2. Type your TikTok name.
  3. Optional: log in to Spotify (step 4) to show the song that is playing on your Spotify, and to let viewers add songs to your Spotify queue.
  4. Copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • Songs only go into your Spotify queue with Spotify Premium, with Spotify open and playing on a phone or computer, and with the Frills page open in a browser tab while you stream.
  • While Frills is in testing, Spotify lets only a short list of people log in. If Spotify says you are not registered, tell us on the feedback page. Spotify allows only a few people, so we can add you only if there is room. Song requests without Spotify work for everyone.
  • It is for TikTok LIVE chat and it is in beta. See what else you can add on the overlays page.
