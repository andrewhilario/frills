About this overlay

A top gifters and likers overlay is a see-through web page with a live ranking that takes turns: first the people who sent the most gifts, then the people who tapped the most likes, then back again. Both rankings move as people send. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick a look and press the test buttons to watch the rankings fill, move and take turns.
  2. Choose which rankings to show, how many seconds each one stays, and how many people show.
  3. Type your TikTok name.
  4. Copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • Gifters are ranked by the diamonds each person sent, and likers by the likes each person tapped.
  • It counts what it sees from the moment it connects. If the page reloads the rankings start again, unless you turn on remember, which keeps them in your own browser or streaming app.
  • Turn one ranking off and the overlay shows only the other, with no turns. That is a plain top gifters list, or a plain top likers list.
  • A ranking with nobody on it yet is skipped while the other one has people, so the overlay does not spend half its time showing an empty list.
  • It is for TikTok LIVE only, because Twitch has no likes and its gifts are subs and bits. It is in beta.
  • Want a celebration when your top liker passes a milestone? That is the top likers overlay. See what else you can add on the overlays page.

How does the top gifters ranking work?

It adds up the diamonds of every gift it sees from each person and ranks them, best first. A gift counts when it is sent (a streak of the same gift counts once it ends, with its final number).

Can I show only the top gifters?

Yes. Turn off Show the top likers and the overlay shows only the gifters, with nothing to rotate.

Does the rotating ranking work on Twitch?

No. It is for TikTok LIVE only for now.
