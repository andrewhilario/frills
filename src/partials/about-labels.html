About this overlay

Rotating stream highlights keep your newest supporters front and center in one compact, see-through Browser source instead of cluttering your screen with five separate widgets. On TikTok LIVE it cycles through your Last gift (showing what gift arrived and who sent it), Last follower, and Top gifter. On Twitch it rotates through your Last follower, Last sub, Last bits, Top sub gifter, and Top bit sender.

How to use it

  1. Pick your overlay look and choose from 12 highlight designs including Highlight card, Ticker bar, Pill, Glass, Neon, macOS, Shell terminal, Discord card, Kawaii, Ribbon, Polaroid, and Minimal.
  2. Toggle which highlights appear in the rotation, customize each slide’s label text, and set how many seconds each highlight stays visible before rotating.
  3. Select TikTok or Twitch in Step 3 and type your channel name. On Twitch, subs, gifted subs, and bits work without logging in; if you also want Last follower on Twitch, log in once below Step 3 and tick the follow switch.
  4. Copy your overlay link in Step 4 and add it to OBS Studio or TikTok LIVE Studio as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • Whenever a brand-new follow, gift, sub, or bit cheer arrives, the overlay jumps immediately to show that supporter, then resumes rotating on your schedule.
  • Empty slots are skipped once your stream has activity, so viewers never wait on blank slides. Turn on Remember highlights if the page reloads to keep latest and top records in this browser or OBS cache.
  • Pair this ticker with the full Top gifters leaderboard or Stream alerts from the overlays hub.

What rotates on TikTok LIVE compared to Twitch?

On TikTok LIVE, the overlay tracks your Last gift (with the gift’s picture, diamond value, and sender’s name), Last follower, cumulative Top gifter, and Top liker (whoever tapped the most likes since the overlay connected). On Twitch, it cycles through your Last follower, Last sub (including resub month counts), Last bits, Top sub gifter, and Top bit sender.

How do I show Last Follower on Twitch?

Twitch does not send new followers over public chat, so subscriber, gifted sub, and bit slides work immediately with no login, while Last Follower uses Frills’ optional one-click Twitch follow listener inside Step 3 (or the standalone Twitch follows page). Only new follower names pass to your overlay link.

Can I turn off specific cards or change the timing?

Yes. Every slot has its own on/off switch and custom heading label in Step 2, and you can tune the rotation speed anywhere from 3 to 30 seconds along with Slide, Pop, or Fade transitions.
