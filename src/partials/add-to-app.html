Add it to your streaming app

In OBS or Streamlabs, add a Browser source and paste your link. In TikTok LIVE Studio, add a source and choose the link or web page choice (the TikTok guide says what we know about Studio). Make the source as big as your scene: the page is see-through, and the overlay sits where you put it inside it. The setup guide shows each step, and the test guide shows how to check it before you go LIVE.
