About this overlay

The top gifters overlay puts a live leaderboard on your stream showing who supported your broadcast the most, sliding names cleanly into their new places whenever a gift, gifted sub, or bit cheer arrives. On TikTok LIVE it adds up each viewer’s total gift diamonds; on Twitch you can rank top sub gifters, top bit senders, or both together in one list. It is free, lightweight, and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick one of Frills’ eight starting looks, choose from 11 leaderboard designs (Classic, Spotlight #1, Compact, Pill, Glass, Neon, macOS, Shell, Kawaii, Minimal, or Stacked minimal), and tweak colors, corners, fonts, and seasonal decorations.
  2. Choose how many supporters appear (from 3 to 10), whether gold, silver, and bronze badges show for the top 3, and how often a crown milestone celebration plays.
  3. In Step 3, choose TikTok or Twitch and enter your channel name. On Twitch, switch Step 2 between Sub gifters, Bit senders, or Both together.
  4. Copy your Browser source link in Step 4 and paste it into OBS Studio or TikTok LIVE Studio.

Good to know

  • Like Top likers, the leaderboard counts what it receives from the moment the Browser source connects.
  • Turn on Remember the totals if the page reloads if you want your OBS or browser source to keep running totals in local storage across restarts or multi-day subathons.
  • On Twitch, both gifted subs and cheered bits arrive directly over public Twitch chat with no login required.
  • For Twitch combined rankings, each gifted sub counts as 500 bits equivalent for sorting, while each row clearly lists both their gifted subs and total bits.
  • Want a compact cycling ticker instead? Try the Rotating highlights overlay, or explore everything in the overlays hub.

How does the Top Gifters leaderboard count gifts?

On TikTok LIVE, each finished gift streak adds its total diamonds to that viewer’s running total and moves their row smoothly whenever they climb the leaderboard. On Twitch, community and single gifted subs add sub counts and cheered bits add bit totals from the moment the overlay connects.

Does Top Gifters work on Twitch without logging in?

Yes. Twitch chat natively broadcasts gifted subs and bit cheers over its public connection, so Top Sub Gifters, Top Bit Senders, and combined leaderboards work immediately as soon as you type your Twitch channel name and copy your link.

When does the milestone crown celebration appear?

Whenever a supporter’s tracked total crosses a multiple of your chosen milestone threshold—such as every 1,000 diamonds or bits, or every 5 gifted subs—their profile picture, name, and score zoom in with confetti and a gold crown. You can trigger it only for the #1 gifter, for anyone on the leaderboard, or switch celebrations off completely.
