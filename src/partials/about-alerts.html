About this overlay

An alerts overlay is a see-through web page that pops up a card whenever something happens on your stream. On TikTok it shows follows, gifts and shares. On Twitch it shows subs, resubs, gifted subs, bits and raids, and follows too once you log in on one private page. Choose Both to combine TikTok and Twitch alerts in one queue from a single link. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick a look and a Card style: Simple, a trading card, a polaroid, a ticket, a sticky note and seven more.
  2. Write the words, choose the sounds and effects, and press the test buttons to see each kind of alert.
  3. Pick TikTok, Twitch, or Both and type your channel name(s).
  4. Copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • On TikTok, follows, gifts and shares only come while your account is LIVE and someone actually does them. Use the test buttons to check how it looks first.
  • You can upload your own picture or GIF (up to 1 MB) and your own sound (up to 300 KB) for each alert. You have to tick that you have the right to use the file.
  • Alerts are in beta. The alerts guide shows how to set them up and test them without going LIVE.

What events does the alerts overlay show on TikTok LIVE and Twitch?

On TikTok LIVE it shows new followers, gifts (with the gift’s picture and diamond tiers) and stream shares. On Twitch it shows subscriptions, resubs, gifted subs, cheers with bits, raids, and new followers when you connect the Twitch follows bridge.

Can I upload my own GIFs and alert sounds?

Yes. Every alert type lets you pick from built-in sounds and visual effects or upload your own PNG, JPEG, WebP or animated GIF (up to 1 MB) and MP3, OGG or WAV sound (up to 300 KB), with a separate image and sound for big gifts.

What happens when many viewers follow or gift at the same time?

Gifts always take priority over follows and shares so a gift is never missed, and three or more follows or shares waiting in line are grouped into a single crowd alert.
