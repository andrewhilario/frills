About this overlay

An alerts overlay is a see-through web page that pops up a card whenever something happens on your LIVE. On TikTok it shows follows, gifts and shares. On Twitch it shows subs, resubs, gifted subs, bits and raids, and follows too once you log in on one private page. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick a look and a Card style: Simple, a trading card, a polaroid, a ticket, a sticky note and seven more.
  2. Write the words, choose the sounds and effects, and press the test buttons to see each kind of alert.
  3. Pick TikTok or Twitch and type your name.
  4. Copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • On TikTok, follows, gifts and shares only come while your account is LIVE and someone actually does them. Use the test buttons to check how it looks first.
  • You can upload your own picture or GIF (up to 1 MB) and your own sound (up to 300 KB) for each alert. You have to tick that you have the right to use the file.
  • Alerts are in beta. The alerts guide shows how to set them up and test them without going LIVE.
