About this overlay

The Frills Stream Outline is a purely visual, parametric border & layout designer built for 9:16 TikTok LIVE, 16:9 OBS/Twitch, and standalone webcam/VTuber frames. Punch crisp transparent holes for your gameplay, facecam, and Frills widgets (Chat, Gift Jar, Goal Bar, Spin the Wheel)—or switch the side slot into a TikTok Gift Rewards menu or Commands & Rules board. Zero heavy video loops, zero chat relay slots, and no account required for the free version.

How to use it

  1. Pick a mode in Step 1 (Vertical 9:16 TikTok LIVE, Full-Screen 16:9, or Webcam / VTuber Box) or tap a 1-click TikTok 9:16 or Twitch / OBS 16:9 Quick Starter.
  2. Choose what goes in the side slot: a Transparent Widget Box for Frills Chat/Gift Jar/Wheel, a Gift Menu / Rewards board, a Commands & Rules Card, or Hide for a full-width cam.
  3. Tune top/bottom margins, split ratio, frame style, backdrop pattern (including 4 free hand-painted watercolor seasonal skies), mascot placement, top title banner, and gift callout strip.
  4. Paste the Browser source link into TikTok LIVE Studio, OBS Studio, or Streamlabs Desktop—or press Download PNG for a zero-CPU transparent image layer.

Good to know

  • In Vertical 9:16 mode, toggle TikTok safe zones above the preview to verify your cutouts and callout bar stay clear of TikTok’s top host pill and bottom chat feed.
  • Multi-cutout layouts punch crisp transparent holes through the backdrop mat so your game capture, webcam, and Frills widgets sit cleanly behind the frame.
  • With Frills Plus, save a live /o/… cloud link to unlock freeform box drag & resize on the canvas, 4 collector frame styles, custom wallpaper backdrops, orbiting/twinkling animations, up to 10 Gift/Command rows, rotating socials, custom corner mascot uploads, 4K PNG export, 1-Link Master Scene framing, and phone spotlight controls.
