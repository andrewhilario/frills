About this overlay

A chat overlay is a see-through web page that shows your live chat on top of your game. Frills draws yours in a look you choose: colors, fonts, corners, a name tag, little pets on the bubbles, and a small platform icon if one chat reads both TikTok and Twitch. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick a starting look, then change anything you like (the preview shows every change at once).
  2. Press the test message button to see how a normal message, a moderator’s and a gift look.
  3. Choose TikTok, Twitch or both, and type your @name or channel.
  4. Copy your link and add it to your streaming app as a Browser source (some apps call it a web page or link source). The setup guide has every step.

Good to know

  • TikTok chat comes through the free Frills relay, which is in beta and may make you wait for a spot when it is busy. Twitch is read straight from your browser.
  • Turn on Keep messages on screen if you never want messages to fade: the oldest one leaves only when newer ones need the room.
  • Your look and names live in the link itself. There is nothing to sign up for, and the chat passing through is never kept.
