About this overlay

The Frills gift & bits jar puts a transparent, animated crystal jar right on your TikTok LIVE or Twitch stream (or both at once). Whenever a viewer sends a TikTok gift (Rose, Finger Heart, Galaxy, Lion) or drops Twitch bits, a sub, or gifted subs, tokens pour cleanly into the jar, stack inside the glass, and raise the glowing fill level toward your target. Once the jar hits 100%, it celebrates with confetti, sound, and a full-jar badge—then stays full, raises the next target, or empties cleanly for the next round. It is free, lightweight, and needs no Frills account or login.

How to use it

  1. Pick one of Frills’ eight starting looks in Step 1 (Matcha, Blueberry, Bubblegum, Arcade, Sticker, Strawberry, Night, or Latte), then choose from 14 crystal jar designs (Boba cup, Cat jar, Bunny jar, Crystal heart, Star wishing jar, Teddy bear jar, Honey pot, Gachapon globe, Ghost jar, Aquarium bowl, Potion flask, Frog pond, Arcade tank, or Piggy jar) and pick Small, Medium, or Large jar size.
  2. In Step 2, choose whether the jar fills by total diamonds/bits or number of gifts, set your full-jar target, and pick what happens when the jar becomes full (stay full until cleared, celebrate and start a new jar, or celebrate and raise the target).
  3. Press the preview test buttons above to drop small gifts, drop a big gift, fill the jar to 100%, or clear it.
  4. In Step 3, choose TikTok, Twitch, or Both and type your channel name(s) (or frills_demo on TikTok for a simulated stream), then copy your Browser source URL in Step 4.

Good to know

  • Works on TikTok LIVE (gifts and completed streaks), Twitch (bits, subs, and gifted subs with no login), or combined Both multistream mode in one shared jar.
  • Turn on Remember jar progress if the page reloads if you want your OBS or TikTok LIVE Studio source to keep accumulated gifts across browser restarts or multi-day marathons.
  • You can independently toggle the label above the jar, the goal counter text below the jar (e.g. 0 / 1,000 gifts), whether the counter caps at 1,000 / 1,000 or counts past the brim, the rising liquid wave fill, the piled gift tokens inside, the recent gifter pop-up badge, percentage readout, and full-jar confetti celebration.
  • Pair Gift Jar with the Top gifters leaderboard or Rotating highlights overlay on the overlays hub.

How does the gift & bits jar fill up?

Each TikTok gift, Twitch bit cheer, sub, or gifted sub adds either its diamond/bit value or its gift count toward the full target you set in Step 2. As progress climbs from 0% to 100%, both the liquid glow inside the container and the piled tokens rise steadily toward the brim of whichever jar design you picked.

What happens when the gift jar becomes full?

As soon as the jar crosses 100%, the full celebration triggers with your custom message, confetti burst, and chosen sound effect. Depending on your setting, the jar can proudly stay marked FULL until you reset it, clear automatically for a fresh jar while counting overflow, or raise the target by your step amount so viewers keep pushing higher.

Does the gift jar work on Twitch and Both platforms?

Yes. Pick Twitch to fill your jar from Twitch bit cheers, subs, and gifted subs with no login required, or choose Both to let TikTok gifts and Twitch bits & subs fill one shared jar together.

Can I keep my jar progress between streams?

Yes. Switch on Remember jar progress if the page reloads in Step 2 and your running total and stacked gifts stay saved locally inside your streaming app or browser storage without sending anything to Frills servers.
