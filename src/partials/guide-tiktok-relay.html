How it works

TikTok has no official way for overlays to read chat, so Frills reads your LIVE’s public chat for you, the way a viewer would, without logging in to TikTok. A free relay we run does the reading and passes your chat to your overlay. You install nothing, and your overlay link works in your streaming app on any PC.

The relay is a beta with a limited number of spots. When it is full you wait in line and the editor tells you your place. It is a small server, so it can be slow or down at times.

Set it up

  1. Connect your TikTok

    Open the editor. In step 2 TikTok is already picked. Type your @name and press Connect. When you go LIVE your chat appears in the preview. If you are not LIVE yet, Frills waits and connects by itself the moment you are.

  2. Copy your link

    Step 3 gives you a link with your look and your name in it.

  3. Add it to your streaming app

    Add a Browser source (some apps call it a web page or link source) and paste the link. The setup guide has the steps for OBS.

  4. Or add it to TikTok LIVE Studio

    Other streamers’ guides say Studio has an Add source button with a Link choice, which some versions call Browser or Web page. Paste the same link there. We haven’t tried Studio ourselves yet. If the source opens at zero size, stretch it by hand across the scene.

Try it without a LIVE

Type frills_demo as your TikTok name. Frills makes up chat, with moderators, gifts and the odd deleted message, and sends it through the same path as the real thing. It is the quickest way to check your look and your source before you go LIVE. The test guide shows how. Nothing is sent to TikTok.

What shows up

  • Chat. Each message, with the chatter’s name and their own profile picture. Turn pictures off under More options if you would rather not risk showing one a viewer chose.
  • Moderators and gifts. Moderators get their icon. Gifts show as a highlighted line, like “sent Rose ×3”.
  • Deleted messages. Frills passes TikTok’s deletions on, so those messages disappear from the overlay. We have only tried this with practice chat so far.
  • Not shown yet. TikTok’s own stickers and custom emotes show as plain text, and follows, shares and joins are not shown.

Good to know

  • It is unofficial. TikTok could change something and stop it working. When that happens the overlay shows an error instead of staying empty.
  • It only reads. It can’t post, moderate or touch your account.
  • Who sees what. TikTok, the signing service the relay uses to talk to it, and the relay itself see which account is being watched, like any viewer. Profile pictures load straight from TikTok’s picture servers.
  • It is free and keeps nothing. Chat is held in memory just long enough to pass it on. Nothing is stored.

If something looks wrong

  • “Waiting for a spot.” The relay is full. Leave the page open and your place moves up as people finish.
  • “The relay isn’t answering.” It may be down for a moment. Wait a minute and press Connect again.
  • “Isn’t live right now.” Check the spelling of your name, and that you are LIVE. Frills keeps checking and connects by itself.
  • “TikTok’s free connection is busy.” The relay reads TikTok through a free signing service that limits how often it can be used. It waits a minute and tries again.
  • The overlay in your streaming app is empty. Use the &demo=1 trick from the setup guide to see whether the source itself works.
