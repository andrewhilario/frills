About this overlay

A subathon timer is a see-through web page with a countdown. You press Start, and from then on every gift that viewers send adds time, so the more they give, the longer you stream. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Set the starting time, and how much time gifts add. You can give a gift its own time (Rose=10 adds 10 seconds for each Rose) and set a rate for all other gifts (seconds for every 100 diamonds). Follows and shares can add time too.
  2. Press the test buttons to watch the clock and the “+” bump.
  3. Type your TikTok name, and copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.
  4. When you go live, press Start on this page. The buttons here also send Pause, Resume, Reset and add or take away minutes to the timer in your streaming app, within a few seconds.

Good to know

  • The clock lives in the overlay in your streaming app, not on this page, so you can close this page once it has started. If the streaming app is closed and opened again, the clock carries on from where it was: a running clock keeps counting while the app is closed, because it ends at a fixed moment.
  • Open the overlay in your streaming app before you press Start, so it is there to hear the button.
  • Gifts are matched by their name as TikTok writes it (Rose, Finger Heart, Galaxy). A gift that is not in your list adds time by its diamonds, at your rate. A gift sent in a streak of 5 adds 5 times its time.
  • Once the time is up, it stays at zero unless you turn on “A gift after time is up starts it again”. You can set the longest the clock can get.
  • It is for TikTok LIVE only, because Twitch has no gifts in this form. It is in beta.
  • See what else you can add on the overlays page.

How does a gift add time?

If the gift is in your list, it adds that many seconds for each one. If not, it adds its diamonds at your rate: with 60 seconds for every 100 diamonds, a 1,000 diamond gift adds 10 minutes. Both are set in the editor.

What does the Start button do from this page?

It sends a short command to a small box that your overlay checks every few seconds. Only the command and a number of seconds go there, no gifts and no names, and only this browser can send to it.
