About this overlay

A subathon timer is a see-through web page with a countdown. You press Start, and from then on every TikTok LIVE gift or Twitch sub, gifted sub, bit cheer or raid adds time automatically, so the more viewers support you, the longer you stream. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Set the starting time, and how much time TikTok gifts and Twitch subs or bits add. Add a row for each gift that should have its own time: pick the gift or type its name, then how long each one adds (in seconds or minutes). Set a rate for all other gifts (seconds for every 100 diamonds or bits). Follows and shares can add time too.
  2. Press the test buttons to watch the clock and the “+” bump.
  3. Pick TikTok, Twitch, or Both, type your channel name(s), and copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.
  4. When you go live, press Start on this page. The buttons here also send Pause, Resume, Reset and add or take away minutes to the timer in your streaming app, within a few seconds.

Good to know

  • The clock lives in the overlay in your streaming app, not on this page, so you can close this page once it has started. If the streaming app is closed and opened again, the clock carries on from where it was: a running clock keeps counting while the app is closed, because it ends at a fixed moment.
  • Open the overlay in your streaming app before you press Start, so it is there to hear the button.
  • On TikTok, gifts are matched by name (Rose, Finger Heart, Galaxy) or by diamonds at your rate, and streaks multiply cleanly. On Twitch, subs, gifted subs, bits and raids add time straight from public chat with no login.
  • Once the time is up, it stays at zero unless you turn on “A gift after time is up starts it again”. You can set the longest the clock can get.
  • Works on TikTok LIVE, Twitch, and combined Both multistream mode from one shared countdown clock.
  • See what else you can add on the overlays page.

How do TikTok gifts and Twitch subs or bits add time?

On TikTok, if a gift is in your list it adds that many seconds; otherwise it adds time by diamonds at your rate (for example, 60 seconds per 100 diamonds makes a 1,000-diamond gift add 10 minutes). On Twitch, subs, gifted subs, and bit cheers add time automatically using your timer rules.

What does the Start button do from this page?

It sends a short command to a small box that your overlay checks every few seconds. Only the command and a number of seconds go there, no gifts and no names, and only this browser can send to it.
