About this overlay

The Spin the wheel overlay puts a live, animated prize and dare wheel straight onto your TikTok LIVE or Twitch broadcast. Whenever a viewer sends a qualifying TikTok gift, drops Twitch bits, sends a sub, or helps fill the shared community spin meter, the wheel spins smoothly on screen with crisp pointer ticks, then announces the gifter and winning slice with confetti and sound. Multiple spins queue up automatically so back-to-back gifts never cut off an active spin. It is lightweight, customizable across all Frills themes, and needs no account or software install.

How to use it

  1. Pick one of Frills’ starting looks in Step 1, then choose between Arcade wheel, Crystal glass, or Kawaii pastel under Wheel design.
  2. In Step 2, pick a starter pack or edit your own 2 to 12 prizes and dares under Wheel slices, choose whether spins trigger per qualifying gift/cheer or by filling a shared spin meter, and adjust spin duration and celebration sounds.
  3. Use the preview buttons above the wheel to spin it immediately, test a practice gift, or queue two consecutive spins.
  4. In Step 3, choose TikTok, Twitch, or Both, type your channel name (or frills_demo for practice events), and copy your Browser source URL in Step 4.

Good to know

  • Up to 5 consecutive spins can wait in the queue at once, with a live queue badge showing how many spins are lined up behind the current turn.
  • Use the optional Only spin for this TikTok gift name filter if you want only a specific gift (like Rose or Galaxy) to trigger the wheel, or leave it blank so any gift above your diamond minimum qualifies.
  • Switching to Fill a spin meter (team goal) lets smaller gifts add diamonds toward a team total so every viewer can help push the wheel toward the next spin.
  • Turn When not spinning to Show only while spinning if you want your screen clean between gifts until a viewer triggers a spin.
  • Combine Spin the wheel with Gift jar, Alerts, or Subathon timer on the overlays hub.

How do viewers trigger a spin on TikTok or Twitch?

In qualifying mode, any TikTok gift meeting your minimum diamonds (and optional gift name), Twitch bit cheer meeting your bit minimum, or Twitch sub/gifted sub spins the wheel once right away. In spin meter mode, every gift, bit cheer, and sub adds to the bar underneath the wheel and triggers a spin each time it reaches your target.

Can a viewer force the wheel with a specific TikTok gift?

Yes. Type the exact TikTok gift name—such as Rose, Doughnut, or Galaxy—into Only spin for this TikTok gift name in Step 2, and all other gifts are ignored when you only want one signature gift to launch spins.

What happens when several people send gifts at once?

The wheel finishes the active spin and holds the winner banner for your chosen number of seconds, then immediately starts the next queued spin in order so no viewer’s gift gets skipped.

Can I hide the wheel between spins?

Yes. Pick Show only while spinning under When not spinning in Step 2. The wheel fades in smoothly whenever a spin begins and tucks itself away again once the winner banner closes.
