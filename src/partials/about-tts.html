About this overlay

Talking Pet TTS pairs a peeking Frills mascot with a customizable speech bubble that pops up whenever a gift or viewer message plays aloud. It cleans out links, enforces per-viewer cooldowns, and drops gracefully to free browser voices if Frills Plus is not on.

How to use it

  1. Pick your pet (Frog, Cat, Bunny, or Hamster), choose a voice style, and press Gift thank-you or type a test line above to hear it speak.
  2. Choose when the pet speaks: gifts, subs and bits only, a chat command such as !say, both alerts and command, or every chat message.
  3. Pick TikTok LIVE or Twitch and type your channel name in Step 3.
  4. Copy your overlay link into OBS or TikTok LIVE Studio as a Browser source.

How do viewers make the talking pet speak on TikTok LIVE or Twitch?

Viewers either send a TikTok gift or Twitch cheer/sub that meets your minimum setting, or type !say followed by their message in your TikTok LIVE or Twitch chat. The pet hops along with the voice audio while showing the message in a speech bubble.

Is Talking Pet TTS free to use?

Yes. The built-in browser voice mode is completely free with no account, no character cap and no API keys required.

How do I stop chat spam in TTS?

Every message automatically strips links and repeated punctuation, caps spoken length, and enforces a per-viewer cooldown timer that you control in Step 2.
