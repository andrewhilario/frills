About this overlay

A recent gifts & support overlay is a see-through web page that keeps a live list of the newest TikTok LIVE gifts and Twitch subs, gifted subs, bit cheers and raids—separately or combined in one feed. You choose how many items show, how long each stays, the words and the colors. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick a look and press the test buttons to watch gifts, subs, bits and raids slide into the feed.
  2. Choose how many items show, how long each stays, and the smallest gift worth showing.
  3. Pick TikTok, Twitch, or Both and type your channel name(s).
  4. Copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • Works on TikTok LIVE (gifts), Twitch (subs, resubs, gifted subs, bits and raids), and combined Both multistream mode from one link.
  • Gifts and Twitch support events arrive live as viewers send them. On TikTok, gift streaks are shown once when the streak finishes so the list stays clean.
  • The overlays questions answer what to do if nothing arrives.

Does the recent gifts & support overlay work on Twitch?

Yes. Pick Twitch to show subs, resubs, gifted subs, bit cheers and raids straight from public Twitch chat with no login—or pick Both to combine TikTok gifts and Twitch support into one shared feed.

Why is my recent gifts list empty?

Events arrive while your stream is live and someone sends a gift, sub, cheer or raid, and TikTok only shows a finished gift streak. To see the list fill without going live, press the test buttons above the preview. If you are live and nothing arrives, the overlays questions have the checks.

Can I choose which gifts show?

You choose how many items show, how long each stays, and the smallest gift worth showing, so small gifts do not push the big ones off the list.
