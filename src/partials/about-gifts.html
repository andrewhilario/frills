About this overlay

A last gifts overlay is a see-through web page that keeps a list of the newest gifts on your LIVE, so people can see who just sent what. You choose how many it shows, how long each stays, the words and the colors. It is free and needs no Frills account.

How to use it

  1. Pick a look and press Add a gift a few times to watch the list fill and slide.
  2. Choose how many gifts show, how long each stays, and the smallest gift worth showing.
  3. Type your TikTok name.
  4. Copy your link into your streaming app as a Browser source.

Good to know

  • It is for TikTok LIVE only for now, because it is built on TikTok gifts. Twitch gifts are subs and bits, which this overlay does not show yet.
  • Gifts only arrive while your account is LIVE and someone sends one. TikTok sends a streak of one gift in pieces, and only the finished streak is shown.
  • It is in beta. The overlays questions answer what to do if nothing arrives.

Does the last gifts overlay work on Twitch?

Not yet. It is built on TikTok gifts. Twitch’s gifts are subs and bits, which this overlay does not show yet.

Why is my last gifts list empty?

Gifts only arrive while your TikTok account is LIVE and someone sends one, and only a finished gift streak is shown. To see the list fill without a LIVE, press Add a gift in the editor. If you are LIVE and nothing arrives, the overlays questions have the checks.

Can I choose which gifts show?

You choose how many gifts show, how long each stays, and the smallest gift worth showing, so small gifts do not push the big ones off the list.
