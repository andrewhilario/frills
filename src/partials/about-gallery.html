About this overlay

The Gift Gallery rotator is a standalone TikTok LIVE overlay designed to spotlight your gift gallery or interactive gift rewards in one compact Browser source. Instead of filling half your screen with a static price chart, each gift rotates smoothly on its own animated spotlight card with diamond costs, optional action rewards, and live unlock tracking.

How to use it

  1. Choose your Frills look in Step 1 and pick from 7 gallery designs: Showcase (a spotlight pedestal card with a glowing halo ring), Card, Glass, Neon, Kawaii, Pill, and Minimal.
  2. Pick Custom gallery list to curate up to 16 gifts (with optional diamond values and short reward captions like “Sticky note on face” or “Spin the wheel”), or switch to Auto-collect live gifts to collect every unique gift viewers send during your LIVE automatically.
  3. Customize the transition (Pop, Slide, Bounce, 3D Flip, or Fade), idle card animation (Shine & float, Spotlight glow, Gentle float, or Still), and rotation timing between 3 and 30 seconds.
  4. Type your TikTok username in Step 3, copy your Browser source link in Step 4, and place it anywhere in your OBS Studio, Streamlabs Desktop, or TikTok LIVE Studio scene.

Good to know

  • Whenever a viewer sends a gift in your gallery, the overlay jumps immediately to that gift, swaps in TikTok’s official gift picture, plays a sparkle celebration burst, and lights up the card with “Lit up by” and that supporter’s name.
  • Turn on Remember lit-up gifts if the page reloads to keep your unlocked gallery progress saved locally in your streaming app across scene switches or refreshes.
  • Because the Gift Gallery rotator is its own separate overlay, you can pair it freely with Rotating highlights, OBS & VTube Studio actions, or Spin the wheel from the overlays hub.

What is the difference between Custom gallery list and Auto-collect live gifts?

In Custom gallery list mode, you choose the exact gifts you want viewers to unlock (up to 16 gifts, such as Rose, Doughnut, Confetti, Corgi, and Galaxy). Unlit gifts invite viewers with “Be the first!” and light up in gold as soon as someone sends them. In Auto-collect live gifts mode, the gallery starts empty and automatically adds every unique gift viewers send during your stream, showing its real TikTok image, total count, and who unlocked it first.

Can I use this as a rotating gift menu for actions or spin the wheel?

Yes. Every row in Custom gallery list mode includes an optional reward caption field (for example, “Sticky note on face”, “Spin the wheel”, or “Slow-mo replay”). Leave the caption blank if you want a clean classic Gift Gallery, or fill it in so viewers always know what each gift triggers on your stream.

Does the Gift Gallery rotator work separately from Rotating highlights?

Yes. It has its own editor, its own independent Browser source link, and its own look, layout, and rotation speed so you can position your Gift Gallery in one corner while keeping Last Gift, Last Follower, Top Gifter, and Top Liker rotating somewhere else.
